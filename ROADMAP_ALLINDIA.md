## 0. Teen fundamental jawab (jo tune poochhe)

### "Exclusivity se meri growth ruk jayegi?"
**Nahi — agar exclusivity ko scope karna aata ho.** Rule:
> **Exclusivity = 1 party × 1 state × 1 saal (renewable, paid rider). Kabhi "all-India forever" nahi.**

"BJP-WB exclusive" dene ke baad bhi UP, Bihar, Assam, MP... 27 state khule hain. Growth exclusivity se nahi rukti — galat-scoped exclusivity se rukti hai.

### "Doosri state mein SAME party ko bech paunga?"
**Haan — aur yahi tera growth engine hai.** Party ka internal network = tera sales channel:
- Purulia district president party WhatsApp groups mein bolega "ye system kaam karta hai" → wahi tera UP/Assam intro
- Party state units ek-doosre se copy karte hain — ek state mein proof = doosri state mein warm entry
- Har election cycle mein party ko har state mein wahi problem hai: shikayat + karyakarta + credit

### "Doosri state mein DOOSRI party ko?"
**Technically haan, practically zeher.** Political vendor ki pehchaan ek party se ho jaati hai. Dono taraf khelne wale pe koi party data ka bharosa nahi karti — aur data hi tera product hai. Ek expose hua "ye to unki bhi seva karta hai" = dono clients gaye.

**Iska sahi solution — DO BRAND strategy:**
| Brand | Kya | Kisko | Party-alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brand A (political)**: "Sahayak CRM" | Grievance OS + karyakarta + booth (andar) | Ek hi party, all-India | Aligned |
| **Brand B (neutral)**: "JanSunwai" | Citizen helpdesk, sirf grievance→department | Municipalities, zilla parishad, koi bhi MLA govt-side, CSR | Neutral, sab jagah |

Brand B alag naam, alag branding, alag entity (baad mein) — DPDP-safe, tender-ready. Brand A ka political data Brand B se **kabhi** mix nahi hota. Ye dono milke "growth cap" problem khatam kar dete hain.

## 1. Market ka size (TAM) — "limited" bilkul nahi hai

| Level | India mein kitne | Realistic price/mahina |
|---|---|---|
| MLA | ~4,120 | ₹20-25k |
| MP (LS) | 543 | ₹40-50k |
| District party unit | ~750+ (per party) | ₹1.2-1.5L (bundle) |
| Municipal corporators/ULB | 4,800+ ULBs | Brand B: ₹30k-1L per ULB |

Ek party ke sirf 15% MLA offices (600) × ₹20k = **₹14.4Cr/saal** — sirf ek product line se. Market kabhi bottleneck nahi hoga; **delivery capacity** (tu + team) bottleneck hai. Roadmap isi liye hiring ke saath likha hai.

## 4. Risks jo is roadmap ko maar sakte hain

1. **Party har jagah haar gayi** → revenue collapse. Bachav: Brand B (neutral, B2G) ko Y2 se hi zinda rakho — target: Y5 tak revenue ka 20-25% Brand B se.
2. **Payment recovery** — political clients late/never pay karte hain. Rule: **quarterly advance, hamesha.** Jo advance nahi deta wo client nahi hai.
3. **WhatsApp ban/policy** — Telegram fallback built-in hai; IVR/missed-call intake Y2 mein add karo.
4. **DPDP/ECI compliance** — Y1 mein basics (ho raha hai), Y3 mein formal audit. Ek scandal = sab khatam.
5. **Founder burnout** — Y1 ka pehla hire revenue ke liye nahi, TERI zindagi ke liye hai. Isko kabhi mat kaato.
6. **Purulia pilot hi fail** → poora roadmap ruk jata hai. Isliye agle 60 din mein sab kuch us ek office pe lagao.
