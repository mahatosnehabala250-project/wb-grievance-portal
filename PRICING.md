*7-agent analysis: market comps (web-researched), value math, cost floor, negotiation design → synthesis → red-team attack → revision. Ye card GTM_PLAN.md ke purane numbers ko REPLACE karta hai.*

## Kyun badla — 3 findings jo sab kuch badalti hain

1. **₹20k pilot COST SE NEECHE tha.** Fully-loaded cost per client ≈ ₹22-23k/mahina (infra ₹2k + founder time ₹11k+ @ ₹900/hr + travel/CAC/tax/receivables risk). ₹25k "base" = zero margin. Tera "sasta lag raha hai" wala feeling arithmetic thi, lobh nahi.
2. **Market comps mein ₹20-25k har cheez se neeche tha** — assembly-seat consultant ₹1.5L/mo (I-PAC-era norm), MLA social media team ₹25-90k/mo, EK hoarding ₹50k-3L/mo. Political money culture mein hoarding se sasta = "serious nahi hai."
3. **Buyer seat bacha raha hai, software nahi khareed raha.** Seat ki value ₹4-8Cr, election spend ₹1.5-2Cr. ₹45k/mo = uske political budget ka ~2%.

Badle mein: demo-visit rights + PA ka 2-line quote (case-study rights nahi — political office kabhi nahi dega). Onboarding 6-6 hafte stagger, kabhi parallel nahi.

### Monthly tiers (per AC)
| Tier | ₹/mahina | Kya |
|---|---|---|
| **Sahayak** (haggler ka floor) | 30,000 | WhatsApp intake + AI categorization + routing + phone dashboard. Booth tools NAHI. |
| **Sangathan** ← YE becho | 45,000 | + booth mapping + karyakarta Telegram + hotspots + SLA + citizen credit-updates + monthly 30-min adoption call (month 1-6) + 3,000 outbound WhatsApp/mo included |
| **War Room** (prestige anchor) | 65,000 | + TV war room + full white-label + founder quarterly review + priority line |

- **Pilot: ₹32,000/mo, GO-LIVE se 6 mahine lock, month 7 se auto ₹45,000 — day-one agreement mein likha.**
- SLA sirf intake/routing/first-response pe — resolution pe KABHI nahi (wo govt ke haath hai).
- **Monthly rate kabhi discount nahi.** Concession ladder: (1) rate hold + value add (War Room TV saal bhar free) → (2) Founding setup route → (3) annual advance = 1 mahina free → (4) "Rate fixed hai, package flexible hai" → Sahayak ₹30k.
- **Day-one cash ask: sirf ₹50,000.**

### Billing
1. Setup 50/50 (signing/go-live); standard clients 100% advance
2. Monthly go-live se; Q1 monthly, **Q2 se quarterly ADVANCE** (PDC)
3. Annual advance = 1 mahina free
4. **Zero balance = system auto-suspend, signing pe hi bata do:** *"System zero balance par khud band ho jata hai Sir — main bhi nahi rok sakta."*

### Exclusivity (do axis, alag-alag)
- **District exclusivity: FREE, likhit, pilot agreement mein.** (Buyer ka #1 dar costless concession se khatam; statewide neutrality bachi rehti hai)
- **Statewide: ₹50L+/saal, 30+ AC minimum.** Khud kabhi mat bolo — wo poochhe tab.

### Election package
- **₹3-5L per AC** (6-mahine war-room window). Har pehli meeting mein anchor ke roop mein bolo ("bookings 2027 se"), close kabhi mat karo. *"Ek AC ke campaign ka 1.5-2% hai."*

### DELETED (bolna bhi nahi)
- ❌ District bundle (₹1.2-2.5L/mo) — us level pe buyer exist nahi karta, aur ye tera apna ₹45k undercut karta tha. 3+ live sites ke baad wapas aayega.
- ❌ ₹2-3L exclusivity rider — ₹1.26Cr/saal ki book ko setup-fee ke daam bech raha tha
- ❌ ₹20-25L district election number spoken pitch se — solo deliver nahi hota, aur Purulia mein quoted numbers ghoomte hain
