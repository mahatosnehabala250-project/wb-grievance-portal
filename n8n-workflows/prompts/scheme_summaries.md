## 1. Kanyashree Prakalpa

**Description:** Financial support for girls' education to prevent child marriage and promote higher education.

**Eligibility:**
- Girls aged 13–18 years
- Family annual income ≤ ₹1,20,000
- Enrolled in a recognized educational institution
- Unmarried at the time of application

**Benefits:**
- Annual scholarship: ₹750 (Class 8–12)
- One-time grant: ₹25,000 at age 18 (if unmarried and continuing education)

**How to Apply:** Through school/college — institution submits application on the student's behalf via the Kanyashree portal.

---

## 2. Lakshmir Bhandar

**Description:** Monthly income support for women heads of household to ensure financial independence.

**Eligibility:**
- Women aged 25–60 years
- Must be head of household or primary caregiver
- Family must hold a valid ration card
- Not a government employee or income-tax payer

**Benefits:**
- General category: ₹500/month
- SC/ST category: ₹1,000/month

**How to Apply:** Apply at Duare Sarkar camps, Block Development Office (BDO), or online via the Lakshmir Bhandar portal.

---

## 3. Swasthya Sathi

**Description:** Universal health coverage scheme providing cashless treatment for all West Bengal families.

**Eligibility:**
- All families residing in West Bengal
- Smart card issued in the name of the woman head of family
- No income ceiling — universal coverage

**Benefits:**
- Cashless treatment up to ₹5,00,000 per year per family
- Covers hospitalization, surgeries, diagnostics, and medicines
- Valid at all government and empanelled private hospitals

**How to Apply:** Apply at Duare Sarkar camps or district health offices. Smart card issued on the spot with biometric verification.

---

## 4. Krishak Bandhu

**Description:** Financial assistance and insurance for farmers in West Bengal.

**Eligibility:**
- All cultivators (owner or sharecropper) with agricultural land
- Must be registered in the state farmer database
- Age 18–60 years for crop assistance; death benefit has no age bar

**Benefits:**
- Crop assistance: ₹10,000/year (₹5,000 per crop season — Kharif and Rabi)
- Death benefit: ₹2,00,000 lump sum to the family of a registered farmer (age 18–60) in case of death

**How to Apply:** Register at Block Development Office or Krishak Bandhu portal with land documents (RoR/Parcha) and Aadhaar.

---

## 5. Rupashree Prakalpa

**Description:** One-time financial grant for marriage of girls from economically weaker families.

**Eligibility:**
- Bride must be 18+ years at the time of marriage
- Family annual income ≤ ₹1,50,000
- Must be a resident of West Bengal
- Only for the first marriage

**Benefits:**
- One-time grant: ₹25,000 (transferred directly to the bride's bank account)

**How to Apply:** Apply at the Block Development Office (BDO) before the marriage date with income certificate, age proof, and bank details.

---

## 6. Sabuj Sathi

**Description:** Free bicycles for students to reduce school dropout rates and improve attendance.

**Eligibility:**
- Students of Class 9–12 in government or government-aided schools
- No income criterion

**Benefits:**
- One free bicycle per eligible student

**How to Apply:** Distributed through schools — no separate application needed. School submits the student list.

---

## 7. Yuvashree

**Description:** Monthly stipend for unemployed youth to support them while seeking employment.

**Eligibility:**
- Youth aged 18–45 years
- Registered at a government employment exchange
- Unemployed and actively seeking work

**Benefits:**
- Monthly stipend: ₹1,500

**How to Apply:** Register at the nearest Employment Exchange office with educational certificates and identity proof.

---

## 8. Taruner Swapna

**Description:** Free tablets/laptops for higher education students to bridge the digital divide.

**Eligibility:**
- Students enrolled in colleges and universities in West Bengal
- Distributed based on institutional allocation

**Benefits:**
- Free tablet or laptop device

**How to Apply:** Distributed through colleges — no separate application. College submits eligible student list.

---

## 9. Sikshashree

**Description:** Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC students to support primary and upper-primary education.

**Eligibility:**
- Students of Class 5–8
- Belonging to SC/ST/OBC categories
- Family annual income ≤ ₹2,50,000

**Benefits:**
- Annual scholarship: ₹800 (Class 5–6) to ₹1,000 (Class 7–8)

**How to Apply:** Through school — institution submits application with caste certificate and income certificate.

---

## 10. Jai Bangla

**Description:** Pension scheme for senior citizens, widows, and differently-abled persons without other pension coverage.

**Eligibility:**
- Senior citizens aged 60+ years, OR
- Widows (any age), OR
- Persons with 40%+ disability
- Must NOT be receiving any other government pension
- Resident of West Bengal

**Benefits:**
- Monthly pension: ₹1,000

**How to Apply:** Apply at Block Development Office or Duare Sarkar camps with age proof, identity documents, and bank account details.

---

## 11. Manabik

**Description:** Monthly pension for persons with disabilities to ensure financial support and dignity.

**Eligibility:**
- Persons with 40% or more disability (certified by a government medical board)
- Resident of West Bengal
- Not receiving any other disability pension

**Benefits:**
- Monthly pension: ₹1,000

**How to Apply:** Apply at the District Disability Rehabilitation Centre or BDO office with disability certificate (UDID card preferred) and bank details.

---

## 12. Gatidhara

**Description:** Subsidized transport scheme for students and daily workers to reduce commuting costs.

**Eligibility:**
- Students and workers who commute daily via public transport
- Must hold a valid student ID or employment proof

**Benefits:**
- Subsidized monthly bus/train pass
- Reduced fare on state transport services

**How to Apply:** Apply at state transport counters or online via the transport department portal with student/worker ID.

---

## 13. Nijashree

**Description:** Housing assistance for homeless and economically weaker families to build their own homes.

**Eligibility:**
- Homeless families or those living in kutcha/dilapidated houses
- Family annual income below BPL threshold
- Must own or have access to land for construction
- Resident of West Bengal

**Benefits:**
- Financial assistance for house construction (amount varies by scheme tier, typically ₹1,20,000–₹2,00,000)

**How to Apply:** Apply at Block Development Office or Gram Panchayat office with land documents, income certificate, and family details.
